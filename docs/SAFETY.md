# Indoor Air Safety & Escalation Policy

## 1. Core Principles
- **Indoor is Not Outdoor**: Ambient outdoor AQI does not represent indoor air quality. Never report indoor AQI numbers without validated indoor sensors.
- **Hierarchy of Controls**: Guidance prioritizes Source Control first, followed by Local Exhaust/Ventilation, and then Filtration.

## 2. Critical Safety Invariants
1. **Wildfire Smoke / Extreme Outdoor Pollution**:
   - Do NOT advise opening windows when outdoor particulate matter is hazardous, unless extreme indoor heat or carbon monoxide poses an immediate threat.
2. **Extreme Heat Precedence**:
   - Never advise staying inside an unventilated, dangerously overheated room solely to avoid outdoor pollution. Heat stress takes precedence over ambient particulate exposure.
3. **Carbon Monoxide (CO) Emergency Interruption**:
   - Any report or alarm of Carbon Monoxide immediately interrupts routine planning and surfaces emergency evacuation and helpline guidance.
